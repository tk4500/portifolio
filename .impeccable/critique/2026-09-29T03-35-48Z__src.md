### Priority Issues
- **[P0] Mobile Usability Collapse**: `WindowWrapper` resize handles and snap logic rely entirely on mouse events (`mousedown`, `clientX`). The OS metaphor breaks down on touch devices, leaving them trapped.
  *Fix*: Implement touch-event listeners (`touchstart`) or force all windows into a locked maximized state on mobile viewports.
  *Suggested command*: `/impeccable adapt`
- **[P1] "Empty Desk" Paralysis**: New users evaluating a freelancer want immediate access to work. If they must actively click icons to open windows, friction is introduced.
  *Fix*: Auto-open the "About Me" and "Project Viewer" windows upon the first load so content is immediately readable.
  *Suggested command*: `/impeccable onboard`
- **[P1] Window Control Contrast (Detector)**: Gray text on a red close button (`text-gray-600` on `bg-red-500`) looks washed out and fails contrast standards.
