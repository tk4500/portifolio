# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Freelance contractors and prospective clients evaluating freelance services. They are looking to understand capabilities, past projects, and how to make contact.

## Product Purpose

A portfolio that demonstrates technical capability and professional identity. Success means visitors clearly understand who the freelancer is, what they can build, and can easily find contact information.

## Positioning

An interactive, OS-style graphical user interface (GUI) within the browser. Instead of a traditional scrolling page, visitors explore the portfolio through an engaging desktop simulation (draggable windows, taskbar, system store).

## Operating Context

Viewed by technical or semi-technical evaluators in desktop or mobile browsers during the hiring or vetting process.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Must preserve the interactive OS/GUI architecture (draggable windows, taskbar, system store).
- Must consume and preserve existing JSON data structures for content (e.g., projects JSON).
- Requires internationalization/localization support.

## Product Principles

1. **Demonstrate via Interface:** The OS concept is itself proof of frontend engineering capability.
2. **Immediate Clarity:** Despite the playful OS metaphor, core information (projects, skills, contact) must remain immediately accessible.
3. **High-End Execution:** Every interaction, window state, and motion must feel fluid, premium, and mechanically precise.
